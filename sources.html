Photos and visual guides

Event photographs, IISE and KU logos, and ESMA topic guides were supplied by the chapter. Event captions follow the chapter’s 2025–2026 photo collection. The 2026–2027 kickoff material and subsequent chapter updates inform the chapter team and direction. Annual IISE student membership renewal information was supplied by chapter leadership. Photographs of the AUS conference show American University of Sharjah, not the KU campus.

The topic guides introduce areas of study. They do not replace the university catalog or academic advice. Upcoming chapter activities are announced through chapter channels.

Using this website

This static website does not collect application responses or run analytics. Applications for chapter roles and general chat access take place on Google Forms. General chat requests require a name and KU student ID for review by the chapter team before access is granted. Those services and our social links follow their own policies. Fonts load from Google Fonts when available, with local fallback fonts otherwise.
